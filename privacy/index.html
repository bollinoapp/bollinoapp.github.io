Bollino privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Bollino is a habit tracker for iPhone. It collects no data about you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your habits, the days you marked them done, your reminders and your settings are stored on your iPhone, inside the app and its widgets. We have no server and no account system, so we never receive them.

iCloud backup

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Bollino saves a backup of your habits, history and settings to your own private iCloud database. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; we can’t see or read it. You can turn backup off in Bollino’s Settings, or delete it in your iPhone’s iCloud settings.

Purchases

Bollino Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account.

No tracking

Bollino contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. It doesn’t track you across apps or websites.

CSV export

When you export your history, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

Bollino collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
