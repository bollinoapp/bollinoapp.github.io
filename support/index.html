Bollino support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email bollino.habits@gmail.com. One person makes Bollino and reads every message.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Bollino.

How do I mark a habit done without opening the app?

Tap the habit’s stamp on a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, use the Bollino control in Control Center or on the Action button, or ask Siri: “Mark a habit done in Bollino”.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

You can miss one day a week and keep your streak. A streak never resets without telling you. You can change this in Settings.

I forgot to mark a day. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open the habit and tap the day in its grid.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Bollino backs up to your iCloud. On the new iPhone, sign in to the same Apple Account, install Bollino, and the first screen offers to restore your backup.

How do I restore Bollino Pro?

Open the Upgrade screen from Settings and tap Restore purchase.

How do I cancel Pro Monthly?

In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Bollino Pro.
